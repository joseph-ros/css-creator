About Running Man Vietnam Season 3

The return of the biggest Vietnamese game show sensation.

Running Man Vietnam is the fast-paced, physical comedy game show adaptation of South Korea’s iconic series, reimagined for Vietnamese audiences with thrilling missions, celebrity guests, and unforgettable team chemistry. Since the first season, the show has become a cultural phenomenon by blending high-energy challenges with heartfelt moments, hilarious banter, and stunning outdoor production. Season 3 arrives with even higher stakes, fresh concepts, and refined production quality that deliver polished visuals, cinematic sets, and larger-than-life guest appearances.

Fans love the show for its ability to keep viewers on the edge of their seats while sharing laugh-out-loud moments between teammates. The return of the program brings back familiar faces alongside new energy, highlighting physical missions that test endurance, strategy and collaboration. Every episode is designed to surprise, whether the group is navigating a treasure hunt, escaping dramatic ambushes, or solving clever puzzles across outdoor landscapes. Season 3 sets the stage for greater adventures, celebrity encounters, and the same unforgettable humor that keeps audiences talking.

With stronger storytelling, heightened production values, and a fresh creative vision, this season is one of the most anticipated chapters in the franchise. Running Man Vietnam Season 3 promises an immersive entertainment experience built on powerful chemistry, daring physicality, and the joyous unpredictability that makes this show a favorite for families and fans everywhere.
